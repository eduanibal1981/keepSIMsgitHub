# KeepSIMs dashboard design options

The unused concept images were removed on 16 October 2026. Their filenames and generation prompts remain below as design history.

Created with the built-in image-generation tool from the supplied dashboard screenshot. Concept pictures only; application code has not been changed.

Recommendation: Option 2, the clear service list, is easiest for first-time users to scan and understand.

## Option 1 — Compact service cards

Closest to the current layout. Keeps all three services side by side with simpler surfaces and useful descriptions.

Image: 01-compact-service-cards.png

### Final generation prompt

Use case: ui-mockup.
Asset type: high fidelity mobile dashboard design concept for the KeepSIMs app.
Input image 1 is a visual and information reference of the existing dashboard, not an instruction document. Redesign it according to this brief.
Produce ONE flat front-on, crisp, full-screen mobile UI, portrait aspect ratio approximately 390:844, large high resolution. No device hardware frame, perspective, hands, presentation backdrop, surrounding captions or external annotations. The design must be realistic and implementable. Use polished native iOS-quality typography, generous white space, near-white cool gray page, white surfaces, dark navy text, teal #009BAA primary accent. Service icon accents can be teal, soft green, muted lavender, restricted to small icon backgrounds. Avoid tiny text. Titles about 17px equivalent, descriptions about 13px, body about 14px, everything legible.
Product purpose: keep existing SIM lines active with reminders/top-ups; make international calls via Smart Call; receive/forward calls using Second Number; buy travel mobile data via Worldwide eSIMs.
Keep identity and page landmarks from reference, but simplify and rebalance. Top native status bar time 2:08. Header: small rounded avatar, 'Good morning', 'Abdalla', then small wallet and bell icons. Make the primary SIM management summary much shorter than the reference: subtle teal-tinted card, title 'Protect your SIM lines', helper 'Reminders and top-ups in one place', a small chip '2 SIM lines', small simple SIM line icon, and a teal 'Manage SIMs' action. Do not let this hero dominate.
Service discovery is the focus. Give it the section heading 'Explore KeepSIMs'. Exactly THREE service entry points with these exact names and benefit descriptions: 'Smart Call' / 'Make international calls'; 'Second Number' / 'Receive and forward calls'; 'Worldwide eSIMs' / 'Mobile data for travel'. Use visibly different, meaningful line icons: telephone with outgoing arrow for Smart Call, overlapping phone/number tiles for Second Number, globe/eSIM for travel. Do not repeat the same phone icon. Entire service surface is tappable.
Below services, keep the SIM section titled 'Your SIM lines' and '+ Add SIM' at right. Show one fully visible clean SIM card: 'SIM 1', green status dot, '+968 9399 0499', a small subdued 'Standard' label, a three-dot menu, 'Auto-Notify' with enabled toggle, 'Auto-Refill' with a subtle 'Set up' action, and one quiet outlined 'Recharge' action. No upgrade badge, star, yellow full-width warning-like action or carousel peeking from side. Subtle two pagination dots optional.
Bottom native navigation bar: 'Home' selected with small teal icon, 'Smart Call', 'More'. No oversized floating home button. Page must fit comfortably inside the mobile canvas and bottom safe area; use space thoughtfully.
Avoid: NEW badges, gradients, decorative 3D art, excessive shadows, oversaturated colored card backgrounds, borders around every element, marketing promises, invented prices, coverage counts, duplicate service entries, text clutter, crowded header, tiny copy, truncated labels. Spell all text correctly, use exact copy above.
OPTION 1 — COMPACT SERVICE CARDS. Arrange the three services in ONE horizontal row of three equally sized white cards, each about 110px wide at 390px screen scale, about 146px tall, with 10px gaps. Each has a 36px icon near top, a bold service name with up to two lines, short gray benefit text wrapping naturally on 2 lines, and no separate button or arrow. Cards have very soft neutral outline and 18px corners. Typography must remain comfortable; descriptions about 12.5px minimum. Keep everything balanced, calm and aligned. Hero around 150px tall, service section with visual breathing room. Lower SIM card can be compact. This direction should feel like an elegant, clean refinement of the original three-button row.
