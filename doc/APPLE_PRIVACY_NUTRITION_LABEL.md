# Apple App Store Privacy Nutrition Label Answers

Use these exact answers when completing the **App Privacy** section in App Store Connect for KeepSIMs.

---

## Data Used to Track You
* **Data Used to Track You:** None. KeepSIMs does not track users across third-party apps or websites.

---

## Data Linked to You

### 1. Contact Info
* **Email Address:** Used for App Functionality, Account Management.
* **Phone Number:** Used for App Functionality (SIM Line Protection & Offline Refill).

### 2. Financial Info
* **Payment Info & Purchase History:** Used for App Functionality, Financial Auditing.

### 3. Location
* **Coarse Location (Country/Region):** Used for App Functionality (Country Carrier Rule Matching).

### 4. Identifiers
* **User ID & Device ID:** Used for App Functionality, Push Notifications.

---

## Data Not Linked to You
* **Diagnostics & Crash Logs:** Used for Analytics, Performance Optimization.

---

## Biometric Data Declaration
* **Data Types:** None. Biometrics (`local_auth`) are handled exclusively by Apple's LocalAuthentication framework on-device and are **never** declared as collected data.
