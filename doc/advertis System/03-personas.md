# 03 — Personas

Six segments. **Digital nomads are deferred to Phase 2** — near-irrelevant to a GCC-anchored launch; do not build creative, landing pages, or targeting for them. The Advertising Manager Guide's nomad sections are superseded for this plan.

Plan assignments below already reflect the coverage constraints in 04: Premium is only sellable for Pakistani, Bangladeshi, and Egyptian (Vodafone/Etisalat) SIMs.

## 1. Indian white-collar professional — UAE / Saudi

- **Job-to-be-done:** keep the India number that anchors UPI, Aadhaar-linked services, bank OTPs, DigiLocker, and tax access alive without thinking about it.
- **Trigger moment:** an OTP that never arrives — bank login, UPI payment, Aadhaar update — or a recharge-validity SMS noticed too late; annual India trip planning ("is my number still alive?").
- **Objection that stops payment:** "My carrier's own app / a relative topping up already covers this — why pay a service fee?"
- **Plan:** **Standard + scheduled refills** (Premium blocked for India until the production-catalog token in the verification register closes). Highest-ARPU segment via *multi-line attach* (own number + spouse + parents), not via Premium.
- **Two cheapest channels:** (1) Kerala/Tamil/Telugu diaspora WhatsApp and Facebook association groups (Malayalam first-class); (2) referral from existing users — this segment refers colleagues.

## 2. South Asian blue-collar worker — Saudi / Qatar / UAE (Pakistan, Bangladesh; India labour segment; Nepal excluded — not live)

- **JTBD:** keep the home number tied to bKash (BD) / Raast, JazzCash, NADRA verification (PK) and the family's ability to reach them; spend as little as possible doing it.
- **Trigger:** hearing a co-worker lost his number and with it his mobile-wallet access; camp/dorm word-of-mouth; SIM lapsed during a 2-year stint without home leave.
- **Objection:** any upfront cost feels large; distrust of app payments; "I'll just have my brother recharge it."
- **Plan:** **Premium auto-mini-refill for PK and BD SIMs** — this is the flagship Premium segment (see unit-economics: mini-refills of $0.32–0.91 make Premium cheap enough to say yes to). Bengali and Urdu creative quoting the actual yearly price. Indian labour segment: Standard.
- **Two cheapest channels:** (1) WhatsApp community seeding via camp/compound influencers and remittance-queue promoters; (2) exchange-house and hypermarket partnerships (poster + QR at the remittance counter — the exact moment they think about home).
- **Note:** Nepali workers are a visible community in Qatar/UAE, but Nepal has zero coverage. No Nepali-language creative, no Nepal promise, until re-entry checklist passes.

## 3. Filipino OFW — across the GCC

- **JTBD:** protect the Philippine number registered under the SIM Registration Act and tied to GCash; keep the family's reload routine organized.
- **Trigger:** GCash locked behind an unreachable number; deregistration warnings; a fellow OFW's horror story in a Facebook group.
- **Objection:** "I already reload through my remittance app / sari-sari channels — what does this add?" Answer in creative: monitoring + automation + multi-line, not the reload itself.
- **Plan:** **Standard + scheduled refills + multi-line** (Premium blocked — min denominations $4.52–8.28). Position scheduled refills as "set your reload day once."
- **Two cheapest channels:** (1) OFW Facebook groups and community organizations (the most organized diaspora communities online — Tagalog creative); (2) referral — highest referral propensity of all six segments; give this segment the referral program first.

## 4. Egyptian professional — GCC (Levantine deferred)

- **JTBD:** keep the Egyptian number that receives bank OTPs and anchors WhatsApp identity and account recovery; recovery requires physical presence in Egypt, which may be a year away.
- **Trigger:** bank app re-verification fails abroad; Vodafone Cash access; a lapsed line discovered during Eid calls home.
- **Objection:** skepticism toward paying for something a cousin in Cairo "can just do"; sensitivity to being sold a subscription.
- **Plan:** **Premium for Vodafone/Etisalat Egypt SIMs; Standard for Orange/WE.** Creative must be carrier-aware — never promise auto-mini-refill generically for Egypt.
- **Two cheapest channels:** (1) Egyptian expat Facebook groups in Saudi/Kuwait/UAE — Egyptian-dialect Arabic creative only; (2) referral through workplace networks (medicine, engineering, education).
- **Deferred:** Lebanese, Syrian, Iraqi professionals are this persona's future expansion — acute account-access pain, high ARPU — but all three origins have zero coverage. No targeting until Tier 3 re-entry.

## 5. Multi-line family manager — any residence market

- **JTBD:** one person (often persona 1 or 4) keeps parents', spouse's, and relatives' home-country numbers alive from one dashboard and one wallet.
- **Trigger:** a parent's number lapses because "someone forgot"; the mental load of tracking three carriers' validity dates.
- **Objection:** "adding four lines = four fees" — $10/yr must be framed as less than one delivery meal for a year of the whole family's numbers.
- **Plan:** **Standard multi-line + scheduled refills**; mixed-origin households get Premium only on eligible lines. This is the economics segment — the only one whose max CAC (~$10.50) gives any headroom.
- **Two cheapest channels:** (1) upsell inside the product to every active single-line user (zero CAC); (2) family-framed WhatsApp shares — "who keeps your parents' numbers alive?" referral cards.

## 6. SME / labour-supply and fleet SIM manager — B2B

- **JTBD:** keep 10–200 staff and device SIMs active with a ledger someone can audit; stop deactivation losses across a scattered workforce/fleet.
- **Trigger:** a batch of workforce SIMs deactivates and re-issuance costs a week of admin; finance demands receipts for airtime spend.
- **Objection:** procurement wants invoicing, VAT compliance, volume pricing, and an SLA — none of which exists yet [VERIFY: B2B pricing and invoicing capability before any B2B promise ships].
- **Plan:** **Standard multi-line at scale**; ledger visibility is the hook (PRD's server-side ledger is a real differentiator here).
- **Two cheapest channels:** (1) direct founder-led outreach to labour-supply agencies, facilities-management and logistics firms in UAE/Saudi; (2) LinkedIn organic content — no paid.

## Cross-cutting

The first wallet deposit is every persona's conversion cliff. Every channel above must carry the 14-day-refund trust line (see creative-brief). Android-first for personas 2 and 3; iOS share matters only for personas 1, 4, 6.
