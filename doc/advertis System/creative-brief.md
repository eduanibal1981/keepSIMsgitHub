## 1. The messaging spine — "Your number is your key"

The strongest pain is NOT "my SIM expired" and NOT "I can't call my family." It is **loss of the identity and money layer tied to the number**:

| Origin | What the number unlocks (use these, nothing invented) |
|---|---|
| India | UPI, Aadhaar-linked services, bank OTPs, DigiLocker, tax portal access |
| Philippines | GCash, SIM Registration Act — a lapsed SIM means deregistration |
| Bangladesh | bKash, bank OTPs |
| Pakistan | Bank OTPs, Raast, NADRA verification |
| Egypt | Bank OTPs, Vodafone Cash, WhatsApp identity, account recovery |
| Sri Lanka | Bank OTPs, WhatsApp identity, account recovery |

Recovery almost always requires **physical presence in the home country** — that is the line that lands with someone two years from their next trip home. Everything builds on "your number is your key." Family communication is the secondary, warmer angle — support, never lead.

Do NOT use identity-layer claims for origins not in this table. Nepal, Lebanon, Iraq, Syria: no creative of any kind (not live — see §3).

## 5. Tone rules

Calm, reliable, international, practical. No fear-based or exaggerated claims. **Never imply the user has already lost their number.** No "last chance," no countdown-to-deactivation mechanics, no claims of guaranteed prevention. Banned claim list from 12-compliance applies to every asset: no universal carrier support, no guaranteed refill success, no carrier affiliation, no stated inactivity thresholds as fact (use "carrier rules vary — we track them for you"), no unverified regions.

## 6. The 14-day refund is a conversion asset

First wallet deposit is the biggest conversion bottleneck. "Unused balance refundable within 14 days" is a **trust reducer of deposit anxiety, not just a legal term**:

- Test it as a headline element on deposit screens and retargeting creative ("Try it. Unused balance refundable for 14 days.").
- Disclose it pre-purchase everywhere (also required by 12-compliance chargeback rules).
- Never paraphrase it into something broader ("money-back guarantee" is banned — it implies more than the policy gives).

## 8. Budget logic — RULE (from unit-economics; apply, do not re-derive)

Cold paid acquisition is structurally unaffordable (max CAC ≈ $2.60 single-line vs $10–50 realistic paid cost-per-payer). Therefore:
