# 8 — Channel Strategy

Weighting rule (fixed, from unit-economics): **community, referral, partnership and organic get the depth; paid platforms get tight, retargeting-scoped treatment.** Budget under $5,000/month: 45% community seeding & micro-creators / 20% referral incentives / 20% warm retargeting / 15% Bahrain–Oman creative tests. Any tactic below that stops fitting these rails gets cut, not the rails.

## A. Community seeding — the primary channel (45%, ≈ $2,250/mo)

**Principle:** be useful inside existing diaspora communities; never blast. GCC SMS/WhatsApp marketing law requires opt-in — all WhatsApp presence is admin-invited content, never unsolicited messages (12-compliance).

1. **Community mapping (week 1 deliverable, then living doc).** Per corridor, list the 20 largest active groups: Kerala/Malayali associations and WhatsApp networks (UAE/Saudi — Malayalam first-class per creative-brief §7), Tamil and Telugu associations, Pakistani welfare associations (Saudi/UAE), Bangladeshi community groups (Saudi/UAE), OFW Facebook groups per emirate/city, Filipino church and hometown associations, Egyptian professional syndicate networks (Saudi/Kuwait/UAE).
2. **Entry model.** Approach admins with value, not money first: a free "keep your number alive" explainer in the community's language, a Q&A session, a small sponsorship of an existing event. Paid admin partnerships only where organic entry stalls; cap any single group at $150/mo.
3. **Content cadence.** Two seedable assets/week/corridor from the creative library: one identity-stake story ("the OTP that never came"), one utility post (status screenshot norm: "checked my lines today — all green"). Persona-correct language and plan per creative-brief §2/§3 — no Premium mention in Indian or Filipino groups.
4. **Micro-creators.** 5–10k-follower diaspora creators demonstrating a real workflow (Guide §5): add line → see status → schedule refill. Budget $100–300/creator; 4–6/month rotating corridors. Blue-collar segments: dorm/camp voice-note influencers matter more than Instagram faces.
5. **Diaspora radio.** Malayalam and Tagalog GCC radio slots are cheap and precisely targeted; one test buy/quarter within the 45% envelope, with a distinct promo code for measurement.
6. **Pre-departure (origin-side).** Seed content into pre-departure orientation channels — Kerala/Manila/Dhaka/Islamabad recruitment-agency groups: "Before you fly: how your number stays alive while you're away." Costs almost nothing; reaches people at the exact moment of relevance.

## B. Referral (20%, ≈ $1,000/mo incentive pool)

Mechanics per section 7. Rails: wallet-credit only [V17 gate], ≤ $1.50 effective CAC, OFW-first rollout, family-framed default card. The pool caps itself: at $1/successful referral pair, $1,000 funds ~500–1,000 referred users/month — more volume than any paid channel could buy at 10× the price.

## C. Partnerships (negotiated outside the media budget where possible)

- **Exchange houses / remittance counters:** poster + QR at the counter — the one physical location where every persona thinks about home monthly. Rev-share or flat placement. Copy discipline is absolute here: the environment screams "money transfer," so the creative must scream "airtime, not money" (banned-terms list).
- **Hypermarkets & telecom resellers:** shelf-talker/QR at the top-up card rack (Lulu-type chains, phone shops in worker districts): "This card tops up once. This app keeps the number alive all year."
- **Employers / labour-supply firms:** doubles as SME pipeline (persona 6) — a welfare benefit ("we keep your workers' home numbers active") that sells the B2B dashboard [V16 gate on B2B promises].
- No airline/telco co-branding attempts at this budget; revisit at scale.

## D. Organic owned: SEO + ASO

- SEO per seo-plan.md — corridor-clustered, high-intent queries ("keep SIM active abroad" family, Guide §5); it is the only channel that compounds. Malayalam and Tagalog tracks included.
- ASO: store listings per residence market; screenshot set leads with status dashboard + two-line view (multi-line norm); no "0% fees" in store copy until [V7] closes. Localized store creative follows creative-brief §7 order.

## E. Paid platforms — tight, retargeting-scoped only

Global rules: warm audiences only (site visitors, installers-no-line, line-no-funding), plus the Bahrain–Oman creative-test cell. No cold prospecting campaigns anywhere. Financial-services classification may require pre-approval per platform [V14] — submit one representative creative per platform before the calendar depends on it. Kill rule (section 9) applies weekly.

| Platform | Permitted use | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Meta (FB/IG) | Warm retargeting; BH/OM tests; lookalike ONLY of depositors and ONLY inside the 15% test envelope | Corridor-language ad sets; exclude paying users; expect Financial Products category friction [V14] |
| Google | Brand + high-intent search ("keep SIM active abroad" cluster) capped small; App campaigns OFF (cold by design) | Search here is intent-harvesting, not prospecting — it clears CAC where SEO hasn't ranked yet; per-keyword CAC watch |
| TikTok | BH/OM creative tests only | Learning volume at <$750/mo is marginal — treat as creative lab, not acquisition |
| Snapchat | Optional BH/OM test slot (strong Saudi reach for future) | Only if Meta tests saturate the 15% envelope; otherwise skip |

**Bahrain–Oman test-bed protocol:** residents targeted about their *origin* SIMs (constraint 4 — local-SIM promises don't exist there and aren't needed). Purpose: cheap statistical reads on hooks/languages before those creatives go to *community* distribution in Saudi/UAE — winners graduate to seeding assets, not to cold scaling.

## F. Explicitly not in this plan

Cold paid social at scale; influencer mega-deals; OOH; app-install campaigns; Nepal/Lebanon/Iraq/Syria anything; Nepali or Levantine-Arabic assets; local-SIM promises outside UAE (du/Etisalat) and Saudi-STC.
