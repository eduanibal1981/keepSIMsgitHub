KeepSIMs Account Deletion Request

In compliance with Google Play and Apple App Store policies, KeepSIMs users can request full deletion of their account and associated data at any time.

Option 1: In-App Instant Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open the KeepSIMs mobile application on your device.
  2. Navigate to Settings → Data & Privacy.
  3. Scroll down to the Danger Zone and tap Delete Account.
  4. Complete identity confirmation. Your account will be frozen immediately and permanently purged in 14 days.

Option 2: Web Deletion Request (Without App)

If you no longer have access to the app, send an email from your registered email address to:

privacy@keepsims.com

Subject line: Account Deletion Request

Please include your registered phone number or email address. Our privacy team will process your request within 48 hours.

Data Retention Summary

Upon account deletion, all operational data (SIM lines, schedules, notifications, push tokens) will be permanently erased. Financial ledger records will be retained in an anonymised format for statutory tax compliance (7 years) with your personal identity permanently detached.
