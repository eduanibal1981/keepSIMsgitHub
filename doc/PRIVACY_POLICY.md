# KeepSIMs Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** July 25, 2026  
**Last Updated:** July 25, 2026  

KeepSIMs ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy and security. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, disclosed, and safeguarded when you use the KeepSIMs mobile application, website, and related services.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Personal & Identity Data
* **Account Credentials:** Email address, user ID, and encrypted authentication tokens managed securely via Supabase Auth.
* **Phone Numbers:** Your mobile phone numbers (MSISDN) associated with protected SIM lines and numbers used for offline refill requests via WhatsApp, SMS, or missed calls.

### B. Device & Technical Telemetry
* **eSIM Information:** ICCIDs, carrier identifiers, data usage metrics, validity periods, and profile provisioning status.
* **Push Notification Tokens:** Device push tokens generated by OneSignal and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to deliver line health alerts and system updates.
* **Country & Location Detection:** Approximate country and geographic zone derived from IP address (via `ipapi.co` and server Edge Functions) to select correct regional carrier rules. **No precise GPS location is collected or stored.**

### C. Biometric Data Protection (Local Only)
* **Local Biometrics (Touch ID / Face ID / Fingerprint / Passcode):** When enabled, biometric confirmation relies entirely on your device's native operating system security enclave via `local_auth`. **Biometric data is processed strictly on-device, is never transmitted off your device, and is never accessible to KeepSIMs servers or any third party.**

### D. Financial & Payment Information
* **Wallet & Payment Records:** Transaction logs, deposit amounts, referral credits, and PayPal capture IDs. Credit card and payment credential processing is handled directly by PayPal; KeepSIMs does not store raw credit card numbers or banking secrets.

### E. Support & Communication Attachments
* Photos, screenshots, or documents submitted voluntarily to technical support (e.g. ticket attachments).

## 3. Data Retention & Anonymisation Policy

* **Operational & Personal Data:** Retained while your account is active. When account deletion is requested, operational data (SIM lines, schedules, notifications, push tokens, ticket messages) is permanently purged following a **14-day grace period**.
* **Financial Ledger Statutory Retention:** In compliance with international tax and legal obligations (including EU VAT, UAE FTA, and US IRS tax standards), financial ledger rows (wallet deposits, plan fee debits, eSIM purchases, PayPal capture references) are **retained for 7 years in an anonymised form**. Upon account deletion, your identity is permanently severed (`ON DELETE SET NULL`), nulling all PII while preserving un-identifiable accounting totals.

---

## 4. Account Deletion & Right to Access (GDPR / CCPA)

* **In-App Account Deletion:** Available under `Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete Account`. Deletion initiates an immediate 14-day frozen grace period during which you can cancel deletion.
* **Web Account Deletion:** Reachable at `https://keepsims.com/delete-account`.
* **GDPR Data Export:** Self-serve JSON export of all your stored data is available instantly under `Settings → Data & Privacy → Export My Data`.
