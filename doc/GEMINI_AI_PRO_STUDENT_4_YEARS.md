## The short answer

Google relaunched its student AI offer on **19 August 2026** (MENA rollout announced **1 September 2026**). There are three different tracks, and only one of them lasts "4 years":

| Track | Who gets it | What you pay | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Free AI Pro (US)** | College students in the **United States** | **$0**, then $19.99/mo unless cancelled | 12 months from redemption |
| **B. Free AI Plus (everyone else)** | Students in **140+ markets** (incl. Egypt, most of MENA/EMEA) — excludes US, Albania, Bolivia, Canada, Hong Kong, Macau, Tunisia | **$0**, then $4.99/mo or local equivalent | 12 months from redemption |
| **C. Discounted AI Pro — the "4 years"** | Students in markets where **Google AI Pro is sold** (excludes US, Canada, Albania, Bolivia, Hong Kong, Macau, Tunisia) | Discounted student rate (MENA: **up to 75% off**; UK: **£4.79/mo**; NZ: **NZ$9.49/mo**) | **Up to 4 consecutive years**, while you remain a student |

The exact wording from Google One Help: *"Eligible students can get a Google AI Pro plan at a discounted rate while you remain a student, for up to 4 consecutive years."* The same 4-year validity applies to the **Google AI Pro + YouTube Premium Student bundle** (US: $8.99/mo vs $35.98/mo for both separately; available in 80+ countries).

**Deadline: everything must be redeemed by 31 December 2026.**
Claim page: `one.google.com/ai-student` · Offer terms: `one.google.com/offer/studentoffer8`

## Who qualifies (checklist)

You must tick **all** of these:

- [ ] **18 or older** (terms list 18+; some regional pages quote other bands).
- [ ] Enrolled at a **higher-education institution** in a country where the plan you want is supported.
- [ ] A **personal Google Account** (not a school-issued Workspace for Education account — those are excluded).
- [ ] A **Google Payments account with a qualifying payment method** saved at sign-up (even for the free year).
- [ ] Successful **student verification through SheerID**, using a **valid school email address** and/or a **photo of your university ID card**.

**You do NOT qualify if you are:** part of a Google **family group** (you must leave it first), a Google One member billed through a third party or affiliate (e.g. **Google Fi**), a **Pixel Pass** subscriber, or using a **supervised Google Account**.

Good news: students whose **2025 AI Pro trial already expired qualify again** for the 2026 offer.

## Step-by-step: claim it in 7 steps

1. **Use your personal Google Account** (not the university-issued one). Sign in on the device you'll keep.
2. **Leave your Google family group first** if you're in one — the offer will not be claimable while you're a family member.
3. **Go to `one.google.com/ai-student`** (students) — or `one.google.com/ai` if you want to see the non-student pricing side by side.
4. **Complete SheerID verification.** Most students pass instantly with a school email; otherwise SheerID asks for a **document upload** (university ID photo, enrollment letter, etc.) which is reviewed manually/automatically and can take time. You get **3 attempts**, after which the verification fails and you must start over.
5. **Add a payment method** and accept the offer terms. A card/Payments account is required even though the first 12 months cost nothing.
6. **Claim the offer** and confirm you got the plan you expected (Google AI Pro in the US; Google AI Plus elsewhere).
7. **Screenshot the plan name + the renewal date** in Google One → Settings, and put a calendar reminder 7 days before that date.

## How the "up to 4 years" actually works

- The **free year (tracks A/B)** is a trial: one 12-month window from the day you redeem it.
- The **discounted plan (track C)** is different: you keep paying the **student rate** instead of the standard price, **for up to 4 consecutive years**, as long as you are still a student.
- **Eligibility must be re-verified every year** with SheerID to keep access. If you graduate or leave the institution, the discount ends and the plan **automatically renews at the standard monthly price** (US: $19.99/mo; elsewhere the local price).
- The 4-year clock is tied to your **student status**, not to a fixed calendar — this is why it is described as "valid for up to 4 years", i.e. a typical degree length.

## Do not get caught by the month-13 bill

The offer is **a 12-month trial with a saved card, not a prepaid gift**:

- It auto-renews at **$19.99/mo (Pro)** or **$4.99/mo / local equivalent (Plus)** unless you cancel before the offer period ends.
- The free window starts **the day you redeem**, not on the announcement date.
- Cancelling is done in **Google One / Google Play / App Store** settings — **uninstalling the app never cancels a subscription**.
- If you cancel but were over your storage limit, your files stay safe but you can't add new ones until you delete files or buy storage.

## Risks: what voids the offer (and what can cost you the account)

Google's Offer Terms are unusually specific here: the offer is **non-transferable**, **cannot be resold**, is **individual use only**, and *"if you violate any of these terms, the Offer will be invalid and we may terminate your trial subscription."* Google also *"reserves the right to determine eligibility in its sole discretion."*

That language matters, because a grey market has grown around "student verification". Here is what people try, and what it actually costs:

| Shortcut people try | Why it breaks the terms | Realistic downside |
|---|---|---|
| **Buying a "SheerID verification" or a ready-made student email** from a marketplace or seller | Verification is tied to an identity that isn't yours; the offer is non-transferable and can't be resold | You hand personal/payment data to a stranger; the **annual re-verification fails**; offer invalidated; subscription terminated; money gone, no Google support route |
| **Using a friend's or relative's student email/ID** | The benefit belongs to that student (individual use only) | Their plan and account are put at risk; the mismatch surfaces at the next yearly SheerID check |
| **Buying or selling a whole Google account that already has the offer** | Explicit resale ban; non-transferable | Sellers keep recovery access; account-takeover risk; plan can be voided; data and purchases at risk |
| **Faking enrollment at a cheap online "school" just to pass verification** | Eligibility is limited to recognised **higher-education institutions**, and it must be re-proven every year | Not all providers qualify; retro checks can remove the benefit mid-year |
| **VPN / region spoofing to grab the better market's plan** | Tracks A/B/C are based on the **country of your institution**, not where you appear to be browsing | Plan cancelled; card/region mismatch flags; loss of the free window |
| **Selling "family slots" on a student plan** | The student offer is **individual use only**, and being in a family group **blocks claiming it entirely** | Everyone who paid you loses access with no recourse — and you carried out a resale violation |

**The consequences that actually hurt:**

1. **Plan termination** — the offer becomes invalid; you can be dropped mid-semester at the standard price, or nothing at all.
2. **Storage cliff** — dropping from 5 TB (or 400 GB) back to the free 15 GB leaves your account **read-only**: files stay safe, but you can't add new ones until you delete data or buy storage.
3. **Loss of the YouTube bundle** — the YouTube Premium / YouTube Premium Lite benefit follows the plan; lose the plan, lose the ad-free and offline access too.
4. **No support, no refund** — Google won't help with a plan it considers invalid, and third-party sellers don't refund.
5. **Account-level exposure** — the risk isn't only the subscription: shady sellers collect exactly the credentials and payment context they need to abuse your account.

**The safe version:** if you're a real student, claim the free year and (where your market allows it) the **discounted rate for up to 4 consecutive years** through `one.google.com/ai-student`. If you're not a student, use the five legitimate test routes in `doc/GEMINI_AI_PRO_NON_STUDENT_TESTING.md` — the family split at ~$3.33/person is cheaper than any grey-market "verification" and it can't be revoked.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| "You're not eligible" though I am a student | You're probably in a **family group**, or signed in with a **school-issued** account, or your Google One plan is billed via a third party (e.g. Google Fi). |
| SheerID keeps failing | Use the **school email** first; if it fails, upload a **clear university ID photo** (Google's MENA post explicitly asks for this). You get 3 attempts before the flow resets. |
| I want add-ons | **Not possible on student offers** — Google Home Premium, Google Health Premium, and storage upgrades are unavailable with student plans. |
| I already used the 2025 trial | You are eligible again for the 2026 offer. |

## FAQ

**Does the student plan work on a school-issued Google account?** No — it is explicitly not available on school-issued accounts.

**Can I share the student plan with my family?** The offer terms say it is **individual use only** and you must not be in a family group to claim it. Google's general family-sharing rules (which do share AI benefits on standard Google AI Pro plans) do not override the student offer's individual-use term — check Google One → Sharing after subscribing if in doubt. For the legal non-student route, see the companion article.

**Is the plan really 4 years?** You get the **discounted student rate for up to 4 consecutive years while you remain a student**, re-verifying each year. The 12-month free window is separate and lasts one year.

**What happens if I fake student status?** See **Risks: what voids the offer** above — the offer is non-transferable, cannot be resold, and violating the terms makes it invalid and can terminate the trial. Don't.
